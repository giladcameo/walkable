§03Profile

Profile

I’m an urban planner based in Berlin, focused on mobility and walkability — how people move through a city on foot and by transit, and how the built environment helps or gets in the way.

I’m not a software engineer. I build these maps and tools by pairing with AI: I bring the planning question and the judgement about what’s worth measuring, the AI writes the code, and I direct, review and iterate until it’s right. It means I can go from “I wonder how much of this district is really within a ten-minute walk” to a working interactive map in an afternoon — no brief, no dev queue.

Most of what’s here started as a question about my own city or one I’d just moved to. What’s actually reachable on foot from this address? Where are the S-Bahn trains right now, and which are late? What does a whole service day look like at once? I’m Israeli and I work in Hebrew and English.

Looking for an urban planning or mobility role — in Berlin, or remote within the EU.
